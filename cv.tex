\section{Research}

\heading{Reproducible evaluation of an IDX equity signal pipeline}{2025--2026}
\subheading{Independent research; ensemble ML for the Indonesia Stock Exchange}

\begin{itemize}
  \item Audited an existing evaluation of a LightGBM/XGBoost signal pipeline and found four
        defects that invalidated its reported performance: the final model was trained on
        rows spanning the test window, walk-forward early stopping used the fold's own test
        set, the feature scaler was fitted on the whole series before splitting, and the
        embargo was five calendar days against a ten-bar label horizon with no purging of
        overlapping labels.
  \item Rebuilt the evaluation with purged and embargoed folds, fold-local scaling,
        average-uniqueness sample weights for overlapping labels, and buy-and-hold and
        momentum baselines under identical IDX transaction costs.
  \item Reported result is negative and is the point of the work: the model returns
        $+0.12\%$ / $+7.62\%$ / $+4.73\%$ across three configurations, against $+14.17\%$
        for buy-and-hold and $+20.71\%$ for the index; walk-forward accuracy of
        0.34--0.36 sits below the 0.53 majority-class baseline. The meta-labeler claimed to
        improve risk-adjusted return instead reduced exposure and most of the return.
  \item Committed a full run manifest --- per-file data SHA-256, exact split dates, seeds,
        configuration, environment lockfile, trade ledger and predictions --- so the result
        is re-derivable rather than asserted.\\
        \href{https://github.com/apricitea/project-aurum/tree/main/research}{github.com/apricitea/project-aurum/tree/main/research}
\end{itemize}

\end{itemize}

\heading{Market-regime dating for IDX equities}{2024}

Implemented a censored local-extrema algorithm in the style of Pagan \& Sossounov: candidate
turning points are filtered by strict peak/trough alternation and phases must clear minimum
duration and amplitude thresholds, so no arbitrary fixed lookback window is assumed.
Validated by a test suite and shown to isolate the 2020 COVID drawdown as a distinct bear
phase on BBRI without supervision.

\heading{Causal effect estimation in telco churn}{2023}

Extended an XGBoost churn model (SHAP-explainable) with an inverse-probability-of-treatment-weighting
estimator and bootstrapped confidence intervals, to separate correlation from causation for
product-adoption decisions. Call Center adoption causally reduced churn and increased CLTV;
video and MyApp adoption increased monthly purchase but their CLTV effects were
indistinguishable from zero --- which changes the recommendation from ``push adoption'' to
``fix the product first''.

\heading{Telecommunications CVM analytics and ML lab}{2026}

Idempotent DuckDB warehouse with a data-quality gate that aborts before touching downstream
tables, two independently calibrated risk models (churn AUC 0.823; late-payment AUC 0.628),
and a test that enforces the forward-looking construction of the late-payment label.
