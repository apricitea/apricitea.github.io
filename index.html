Notes

What I'm noodling on

Things that currently live in my notes app and coffee chats.

Agentic AI groundedness

How do you keep an agent from drifting when it runs autonomously over long horizons? I'm running a live experiment: a self-hosted agent stack that observes itself, learns from traces, and proposes improvements. The hard part isn't the loop — it's knowing when to trust the agent's own judgment.

ML under distribution shift

Most ML systems are evaluated on held-out sets from the same distribution as training. In production — especially financial markets — the distribution shifts constantly. Walk-forward cross-validation with embargo is a start, but the deeper question is how to detect shift early enough to act.

Building with Indonesian data

A lot of ML tooling assumes English-first data and deep markets. Working with Bahasa Indonesia NLP and Indonesian equity markets means confronting underrepresented languages, thin data, and regime changes. Constraints force cleaner thinking.
